What we do

An independent app studio in Zwolle

Kali Yuga B.V. designs, builds and publishes utility apps for iPhone: turning photos into coloring pages, cleaning up duplicate pictures, measuring building plans, keeping a family emergency binder. Different jobs, the same three promises.

1

Pay once

No subscriptions and no accounts. Try the app for free, unlock it with one purchase, and it's yours.

2

Your data stays yours

Everything runs on your iPhone. We have no servers, no analytics and no ads — there is simply nothing to collect.

3

Made to be understood

Each app does one thing and explains what it did. The result is something you can check, export and keep.
