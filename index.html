About us

Why these apps exist

Most of them started with a small annoyance. You want to do one thing, once: turn a photo into a coloring page for a rainy afternoon, put the stops of a delivery round in a sensible order, make a list of what's in the house before the insurer asks. And the app you find wants an account, a subscription, and your photo library on somebody's server.

So we build the version we wanted instead. One job per app. A price you pay once. And nothing leaves the phone, because the app has no server to send it to. That last part isn't a policy, it's how they're built. There's no analytics library in there, no ad network, no sign-up screen. If you delete the app, the data goes with it.

Kali Yuga B.V. is registered in Zwolle, the Netherlands. The apps are made here, the support mail is read here, and if something is broken you'll usually hear back within a day or two.
