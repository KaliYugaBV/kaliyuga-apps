GuestKit

A welcome book for your holiday rental that guests will really read.

Coming to the App StoreSupportPrivacyTermsFeedback

Answer the same guest questions once, and GuestKit turns them into the welcome book, the printed signs and the guest page your rental needs. Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no subscription.

GuestKit walks you through eight sections — Check-in & Arrival, House Rules, How Things Work, Kitchen & Supplies, Our Local Favorites, Getting Around, Safety & Emergency, and Check-out. Each one comes with prompts already written for you ("Arrival time", "Quiet hours", "Parking", "Check-out time"), so you fill in blanks instead of staring at an empty page. Add your own topics wherever you need them.

What It Does

  • Guided sections with ready-made prompts, plus your own custom topics
  • A basics card per property: WiFi network and password, host name and phone, address, welcome message
  • Three documents from the same answers:

– Welcome Book — the bound PDF guests read, with an optional contents page and page numbers

– Print Kit — the loose signs for the wall: welcome card, WiFi card, house rules, check-out list, emergency sheet

– Guest Page — one self-contained file you send guests; it opens on any phone and keeps working without a signal

  • A scannable WiFi QR code, so guests join the network with the camera instead of typing the password
  • A4 or US Letter, chosen for you from your region and changeable per property
  • As many properties as you manage — one cottage or twelve apartments
  • A second rental starts from the first: your house rules, your local tips, your name and number all travel across, while the address and the WiFi stay empty on purpose — those belong to the building, not to you
  • Before you share, GuestKit names anything still missing — no WiFi network means no WiFi card, no address, phone or safety topic means no emergency sheet — and takes you straight to the field that fixes it. If your place really has no WiFi, say so once and it stops asking
  • Every property says whether the copy your guests are holding still matches what you have written since
  • Start from a finished example: one tap opens a fully written cottage — book, signs and guest page complete — so you can read the whole thing before you type a word. Make it yours (the made-up WiFi, address and codes are cleared for you) or delete it in one go

Why You'll Like It

  • One-time purchase — no subscription, ever
  • 100% on your device — nothing is uploaded and there is nothing to sign up for
  • No ads, no account
  • Page through all three finished documents — book, print kit and guest page — before you pay a cent

Hosted guidebooks charge by the year, per property, and put your house on someone else's website. GuestKit is one fair unlock. Write everything, add every property and page through the finished documents for free; the unlock turns on generating the Welcome Book, the Print Kit and the Guest Page — for every property, as often as you like.

Answer it once. Hand it to every guest.
